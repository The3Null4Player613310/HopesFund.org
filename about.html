Hope's Fund

In Memory of Hope

    Hope was a beautiful Golden Retriever whose life was tragically cut short by kidney failure brought on by severe dehydration. Her death was preventable. With proper nutritional and fluid support, including the timely placement of a feeding tube when she could no longer eat or drink adequately, Hope may have had a chance to recover and live the long, happy life she deserved. Her story is a heartbreaking reminder that sometimes the right medical intervention, provided at the right time, can mean the difference between life and loss.


    Hope’s death inspired the creation of this charity, established in her memory to help other beloved pets receive the lifesaving care they need. Veterinary treatment can be incredibly expensive, and financial hardship should never have to determine whether a pet gets a chance to survive This charity provides financial assistance to pet owner's facing critical veterinary situations, helping fund essential treatments, nutrition, feeding tubes, and other supportive care that can give vulnerable animals a fighting chance. Hope may not have been saved, but her memory can help save others; and ensure that her life continues to make a difference.
